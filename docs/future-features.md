# Future features

Planned, but not part of the first version. Roughly in the order they're likely to happen.

## Media types

- **Music**: artists, albums and tracks, with a persistent mini-player that keeps playing while you browse, playlists, and lyrics where available.
- **Photos**: browsing by folder and date and slideshows, like Videos libraries already do for home videos, with no online metadata (everything comes from the files themselves).
- **Books & audiobooks**: progress per user, chapters, and a sleep timer for audiobooks.
- **Live TV & DVR**: tuner support, a guide, and recordings that land in a library.

## Playback

- **More subtitle sources**: providers besides OpenSubtitles, and automatic downloads for new videos in chosen languages.
- **Offline downloads**: download (converted) copies to phones and laptops for watching without a connection, with progress synced back.

## Clients

- **Android TV / Google TV app**: a thin native wrapper around the TV layout, so Cinemion installs from the TV's app store and can use the TV's own video decoders.
- **Smart-TV browsers**: a lighter TV layout that works in the older built-in browsers of Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TVs, with IR remotes.
- **Chromecast / casting**: cast from the phone layout to a Chromecast or Google TV, using a registered receiver app.

## Access & integrations

- **Built-in remote access**: reach a server from outside the house without port forwarding, through a relay service on ionnet.io.
- **Trakt scrobbling**: sync watch history and ratings to Trakt, per user.

## Metadata

- **More metadata providers**: TVDB for shows, and OMDb for IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings, on top of TMDB and local files.
